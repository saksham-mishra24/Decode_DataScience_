# 🔢 Scene 4 — N-gram Tokenization (🔥 Very Important)

---

**Aman:**
Ye N-gram kya hota hai?

---

**Riya:**
Ab interesting part 😎

---

## 💡 Idea

```text id="ngram_def"
N-gram = N consecutive words ka group
```

---

---

## 🔹 Unigram (N = 1)

```text id="uni_ex"
"I love AI"
```

👉

```text id="uni_out"
["I", "love", "AI"]
```

---

## 🔹 Bigram (N = 2)

```text id="bi_out"
["I love", "love AI"]
```

---

## 🔹 Trigram (N = 3)

```text id="tri_out"
["I love AI"]
```

---

**Aman (eyes wide 😳):**
Ohhh matlab grouping ho rahi hai!

---

---

```text id="phrase_ex"

```

---

# 💻 Scene 7 — Code Example (N-grams)

```python
from sklearn.feature_extraction.text import CountVectorizer

text = ["I love AI"]

# bigram model
vectorizer = CountVectorizer(ngram_range=(2,2))

X = vectorizer.fit_transform(text)

print(vectorizer.get_feature_names_out())
```

---

## 🧠 Explanation

---

```python
ngram_range=(2,2)
```

👉 only bigrams
