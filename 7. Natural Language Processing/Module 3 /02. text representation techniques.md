# 🧩 FULL EVOLUTION (STEP-BY-STEP)

---

## 🔹 LEVEL 1: Count-Based Methods (Sabse Basic)

---

```text id="level1"
Words ko count karo → vector banao
```

---

### 🔸 Techniques:

#### 1. Bag of Words (BoW)

👉 har word ka count

---

#### 2. Binary BoW

👉 word present hai ya nahi (1/0)

---

#### 3. N-grams

👉 word sequences (2-word, 3-word combos)

---

---

## 📌 Example

Sentence:

```text id="ex1"
"I love AI"
```

---

Vocabulary:

```text id="vocab1"
[I, love, AI]
```

---

Vector:

```text id="vector1"
[1, 1, 1]
```

---

---

# 🔹 LEVEL 2: Weighted Methods

---

```text id="level2"
Important words ko zyada weight do
```

---

### 🔸 Technique:

#### TF-IDF

---

## 🧠 Logic

* TF → word kitni baar aaya
* IDF → word kitna rare hai

---

---

## 📌 Example

```text id="tfidf_ex"
"the" → har jagah → low importance  
"AI" → rare → high importance
```

---

---

## ⚠️ Problem

```text id="prob2"
Still meaning samajhta nahi
```

---

**Riya:**
Bilkul 💯

# 🔹 LEVEL 3: Word Embeddings (Game Changer)

---

```text id="level3"
Words ko meaningful vectors me convert karna
```

---

👉 Ab sirf count nahi
👉 meaning bhi capture

---

---

## 🔸 Techniques:

* Word2Vec
* GloVe
* FastText

---

---

## 📌 Example (Mind-Blowing)

```text id="embed_ex2"
king - man + woman ≈ queen
```

---

---

## 🧠 Insight

```text id="embed_insight"
Similar words → similar vectors
```

---

---

## ⚠️ Problem

```text id="prob3"
Same word → same vector (context ignore)
```

---

Example:

```text id="bank_problem"
bank = river bank = financial bank
```

---

👉 same representation ❌

---

---

# 🔹 LEVEL 4: Contextual Embeddings (Modern NLP)

---

```text id="level4"
Word ka meaning context ke hisaab se change hota hai
```

---

---

## 🔸 Techniques:

* BERT
* GPT
* ELMo

---

---

## 📌 Example

```text id="context_ex"

👉 different meaning

---

👉 model samajh leta hai 😏🔥

## 🧠 Insight

```

# 📊 FULL HIERARCHY (Final Structure)

---

```text id="full_hierarchy"
LEVEL 1 → Count-based (BoW, N-grams)
LEVEL 2 → Weighted (TF-IDF)
LEVEL 3 → Static embeddings (Word2Vec, GloVe)
LEVEL 4 → Contextual embeddings (BERT, GPT)
```

---

---

# ⚔️ REAL-WORLD COMPARISON

---

| Level    | Power     | Speed  | Understanding |
| -------- | --------- | ------ | ------------- |
| BoW      | low       | fast   | none          |
| TF-IDF   | medium    | fast   | shallow       |
| Word2Vec | high      | medium | semantic      |
| BERT/GPT | very high | slow   | deep          |
