# 🎭 Chapter — One Hot Encoding (OHE)

---
- ✔ One Hot Encoding (OHE) = simplest aur safest starting point for text representation
- ❌ Lekin real-world NLP me akela use karoge to model blind ho jayega (no meaning, no context)
---

# 🧠 Scene 2 — Core Idea

---

👉 Har word = ek unique position

👉 Us position pe **1**, baaki sab jagah **0**

---

---

## 📌 Example (Step-by-Step)

---

### Sentence

```text id="ohe_sent"
"I love AI"
```

---

### Step 1: Vocabulary banao

```text id="ohe_vocab"
[I, love, AI]
```

---

### Step 2: Assign positions

```text id="ohe_pos"
I → 0  
love → 1  
AI → 2  
```

---

### Step 3: Convert to vectors

---

```text id="ohe_vectors"
I     → [1, 0, 0]  
love  → [0, 1, 0]  
AI    → [0, 0, 1]  
```

---

---

**Aman:**
Matlab har word ka apna identity vector hai 😳

---

**Riya:**
Exactly 💯

# ⚙️ Scene 3 — How It Works (Simple Logic)

---

```text id="ohe_logic"

```

---

👉 jitne words → utni vector length

## 📌 Example

Vocabulary:


# 💻 Scene 4 — Code Example

---

```python id="ohe_code"
from sklearn.preprocessing import OneHotEncoder
import numpy as np

# words
words = np.array([["I"], ["love"], ["AI"]])

# encoder
encoder = OneHotEncoder(sparse=False)

# transform
encoded = encoder.fit_transform(words)

print(encoded)
```

# ⚠️ Scene 5 — BIG Problems of OHE

---

**Riya (serious):**
Ab asli truth sun 😏

---

---

## ❌ Problem 1: No Meaning

```text id="prob_ohe1"
"I" ≠ "love" ≠ "AI" (sab equally different)
```

---

👉 "king" aur "queen" bhi unrelated ❌

---

---

## ❌ Problem 2: No Similarity

```text id="prob_ohe2"
dog ≠ cat ≠ lion
```

---

👉 sab vectors unrelated

---

---

## ❌ Problem 3: High Dimension

```text id="prob_ohe3"
Vocabulary size = 10,000 → vector size = 10,000
```

---

👉 huge memory ❌

---

---

## ❌ Problem 4: Sparse Data

```text id="prob_ohe4"
Mostly zeros
```

---

👉 inefficient

# ⚖️ Scene 7 — When to Use OHE

---

## ✅ Use when:

* small dataset
* simple classification
* baseline model

---

---

## ❌ Avoid when:

* large vocabulary
* semantic understanding needed
* NLP deep tasks
