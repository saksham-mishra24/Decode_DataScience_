# 🧠 Step 2 — TF-IDF ka Idea

---

```text id="tfidf_def"
TF-IDF = important words ko high score, common words ko low score
```

---

---

# 🧩 Step 3 — Do parts hote hain

---

## 🔹 1. TF (Term Frequency)

---

```text id="tf_def"
Word kitni baar aaya sentence me
```

---

👉 same as BoW

---

---

## 🔹 2. IDF (Inverse Document Frequency)

---

```text id="idf_def"
Word kitna rare hai poore dataset me
```

---

👉 rare word → high importance
👉 common word → low importance

---

---

# 🎯 Step 4 — Simple Logic

---

```text id="tfidf_formula"
TF-IDF = TF × IDF
```

---

---

# 🧠 Step 5 — Intuition (IMPORTANT)

---

**Riya:**

# 💻 Step 6 — Code Example

---

```python id="tfidf_code"
from sklearn.feature_extraction.text import TfidfVectorizer

sentences = [
    "I love AI",
    "I love ML",
    "AI is powerful"
]

# TF-IDF model
vectorizer = TfidfVectorizer()

X = vectorizer.fit_transform(sentences)

print("Vocabulary:", vectorizer.get_feature_names_out())
print("TF-IDF Vectors:\n", X.toarray())
```
