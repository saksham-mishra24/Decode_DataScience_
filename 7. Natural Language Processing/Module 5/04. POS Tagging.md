# 🎯 Step 3 — POS Tags (Detailed)

---

## 🔹 Noun (NN)

---

```text id="noun_def"
Person, place, thing
```

Examples:

```text id="noun_ex"
cat, Delhi, car
```

---

---

## 🔹 Verb (VB)

---

```text id="verb_def"
Action ya state
```

Examples:

```text id="verb_ex"
run, eat, is
```

---

---

## 🔹 Adjective (JJ)

---

```text id="adj_def"
Noun ko describe karta hai
```

```text id="adj_ex"
big, beautiful, smart
```

---

---

## 🔹 Adverb (RB)

---

```text id="adv_def"
Verb ko describe karta hai
```

```text id="adv_ex"
quickly, very, slowly
```

---

---

## 🔹 Pronoun (PRP)

---

```text id="pronoun_def"
Noun ki jagah use hota hai
```

```text id="pronoun_ex"
he, she, it
```

---

---

## 🔹 Determiner (DT)

---

```text id="det_def"
Noun ke pehle aata hai
```

```text id="det_ex"
the, a, an
```

---

---

## 🔹 Preposition (IN)

---

```text id="prep_def"
relation show karta hai
```

```text id="prep_ex"

```

# 💻 Step 4 — Code (NLTK POS Tagging)

---

```python id="pos_code1"
import nltk
from nltk.tokenize import word_tokenize
from nltk import pos_tag

text = "The smart boy runs quickly"

tokens = word_tokenize(text)
tags = pos_tag(tokens)

print(tags)
```

---

---

## 🧠 Output

---

```text id="pos_output"
[('The','DT'), ('smart','JJ'), ('boy','NN'), ('runs','VBZ'), ('quickly','RB')]
```

# 🧠 Step 5 — Advanced Tags (Important)

---

## Verb forms

| Tag | Meaning         |
| --- | --------------- |
| VB  | base form (eat) |
| VBD | past (ate)      |
| VBG | -ing (eating)   |
| VBN | past participle |
| VBZ | present (eats)  |

---

---

## Noun types

| Tag | Meaning             |
| --- | ------------------- |
| NN  | singular noun       |
| NNS | plural noun         |
| NNP | proper noun (India) |
