# 🧪 Scene 9 — Type 1: Average Filter

## 🎬 Entry

👉 Sabse simple filter

---

## 🧠 Kaise kaam karta hai?

👉 Neighbor pixels ka average le lo

Example:

```text
[10  20  30
 20 100  40
 30  40  50]
```

👉 Average ≈ 37

👉 Center pixel = 37

---

## 😳 Scene 10 — Result

👉 Sharp value (100) smooth ho gayi
👉 Noise kam ho gaya

---

# 🌫️ Scene 11 — Type 2: Gaussian Blur

## 🎬 Entry

👉 Smart smoothing filter

---

## 🧠 Idea

👉 Center pixel ko zyada importance
👉 Door wale pixels ko kam

---

## 😎 Why better?

👉 Natural blur
👉 Less distortion

---

## ⚙️ Code

```python
blur = cv2.GaussianBlur(img, (5,5), 0)
```

---

# 🧱 Scene 12 — Type 3: Median Filter

## 🎬 Entry

👉 Noise killer 😈

---

## 🧠 Kaise kaam karta hai?

👉 Values ko sort karo
👉 middle value pick karo

Example:

```text
[10  20  200
 20 255  40
 30  40  50]
```

👉 Sorted: [10,20,20,30,40,40,50,200,255]

👉 Median = 40

---

## 🔥 Result

👉 Random noise remove
👉 edges safe

| Gaussian | Medium | Good    | Natural blur  |
| Median   | Slow   | Best    | Noise removal |
